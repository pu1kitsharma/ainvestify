# Local evidence-bound material diagnostics

The [manifest](manifest.json) records eleven **synthetic, local 9B** draft trials,
their exact request digests, raw-attempt file hashes, results, and failure reasons.
The attempts remain in ignored `runtime_qualification/local_material_harness/`
directories. No private Toffee inputs, accepted investor package, or model weights
are in this evaluation set.

The exploratory v1–v6 contracts were compacted after their raw responses were
saved. An ignored, hash-identified source snapshot retains their uncommitted
implementation; those trials are historical and are not executable under the
compact tracked code. Legacy material contracts and v7/v8 have exact replay.
V7 generated source-bound intro and pitch drafts, but independent content review
rejected unsupported market demand, lost company self-report attribution, and
sparse visual quality. V8 added generic claim-modality and slide-topic checks;
the installed 9B repeated a funding slide with `financial_unknown` purpose
through its final bounded patch, so V8 blocked before pitch.

The purpose-first v1/v2 contracts fix slide purposes from the accepted memo
workflow, while the installed local model authors headings, sentences, source
IDs, and layouts. They extract complete claim/excerpt pairs from the `reader_v2`
memo appendix and offer a source-scoped memo analysis packet. V2 also supplies
the memo's exact financial unknowns and asks the model to write a distinct
`financial_status` sentence. Each deck permits one model response and has exact
request/response replay. The sandbox-only v1 call retained its connection error.
The separate loopback v1 call failed the financial disclosure gate. V2 failed a
product slide length gate; its financial status wording was also insufficient.
No pitch was attempted after these intro failures. The installed 9B has not
qualified for investor materials; these code checks do not establish content,
financial, or visual acceptance.
