# GoCardless — company preparation

Internal AI drafts. Sources are reported claims; proposals have not been executed.

## Research memo

### Business and customer

Status: complete

GoCardless provides a bank-to-bank payment platform letting businesses collect one-off, recurring and instalment payments, and send outbound payments, aiming to replace costly card fees and reduce payment failures and late-payment chasing for merchants. Partners such as Wise and accounting integrations extend its reach across markets.

Sources: [S1](#source-s1), [S3](#source-s3), [S2](#source-s2)

### Revenue mechanism

Status: complete

Revenue mechanism: GoCardless charges merchants a transaction fee (a percentage plus a fixed pence amount) deducted directly from collected payments under its Standard, Advanced and Pro plans. Larger businesses can negotiate a Custom plan, invoiced monthly with volume-based discounted rates. Charities receive a discount on some fees, and UK merchants are separately charged VAT on fees regardless of their own VAT status.

Unknown economics: No records show GoCardless's actual earned revenue, attributable processing or partner costs, or profit by entity and period, distinct from the volumes it merely processes for merchants.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4)

### Opportunity and unresolved risks

Status: complete

Reason to engage: If actual fee revenue and margins scale consistently with the reported hundred-thousand-plus merchant base and stated global processing volume, the tariff-based model could be attractive; this remains conditional on unseen financials.

Unresolved risk: The business depends on Direct Debit and Pay by Bank success rates staying high and on partners like Wise for currency conversion; any decline in either could affect merchant retention and fee revenue, which is not yet evidenced financially.

Next decision: Before any investment view, request audited financial statements; adverse evidence such as declining transaction revenue or thin/negative margins would pause further engagement.

Sources: [S1](#source-s1), [S5](#source-s5), [S3](#source-s3)

## Founder proposal

### Why we are approaching you

Status: complete

Reported observation: GoCardless has publicly cited passing fifty thousand and later over one hundred thousand businesses served, alongside a past $75m Series E funding round and reported global processing above $130bn annually.

Question to explore: How have earned revenue and margins evolved alongside this reported merchant and volume growth?

Sources: [S6](#source-s6), [S5](#source-s5)

### The assistance we propose

Status: complete

Proposed work: Our advisory team would like to help reconcile GoCardless's published per-transaction fee schedule against actual recognized revenue, and separately assess processing and partner costs to estimate contribution margins, both for an agreed fiscal period and entity in GBP. This would produce a revenue reconciliation table and a per-plan margin summary to inform whether growth in processed volume and merchant count has translated into sustainable, well-understood economics worth deeper investment diligence.

Invitation: We'd welcome a conversation with the founders to agree the right period, entity scope, and which records make sense to share before requesting anything sensitive.

Sources: [S6](#source-s6), [S2](#source-s2), [S5](#source-s5)

## Diligence request

### First diligence request

Status: complete

Question: Does GoCardless's actual per-transaction revenue match its published fee schedule?

Records to request: Request a transaction-level ledger and merchant invoices for a named period (e.g. one fiscal year), GoCardless Ltd, in GBP, covering Standard, Advanced, Pro and Custom plan merchants, including VAT treatment and charity discounts applied.

Decision: A close match supports confidence in pricing transparency; a material gap raises questions about revenue recognition or discounting practices that need founder explanation before further work.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4)

### Second diligence request

Status: complete

Question: What are GoCardless's processing costs and margin per transaction by plan?

Records to request: Request cost-of-processing records (bank scheme fees, Wise currency-conversion costs, fraud/failure-handling costs) and the revenue ledger for GoCardless Ltd, same fiscal year and currency as above, split by plan tier.

Decision: Positive, stable contribution margins would support further diligence into overall profitability; unclear or negative margins would require cost-structure discussion with founders before valuation work.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S5](#source-s5)

## Investment-readiness plan

### First readiness action

Status: complete

Action: Apply the full published tariff (percentage, fixed fee, discounts) to the same transactions recorded in the ledger to see whether realized revenue matches the schedule. Differences would flag undisclosed discounting, billing errors, or Custom-plan variance not visible from public pricing.

Output: A reconciliation table comparing schedule-implied fees versus recognized revenue per plan type for the agreed period.

Decision: A close match supports confidence in pricing transparency; a material gap raises questions about revenue recognition or discounting practices that need founder explanation before further work.

Required input: Request a transaction-level ledger and merchant invoices for a named period (e.g. one fiscal year), GoCardless Ltd, in GBP, covering Standard, Advanced, Pro and Custom plan merchants, including VAT treatment and charity discounts applied.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4)

### Second readiness action

Status: complete

Action: Subtract attributable processing and partner costs from recognized transaction revenue for the same transactions to estimate contribution margin per plan. This avoids inferring profitability from list prices or processed-volume growth alone.

Output: A per-plan contribution margin summary for the agreed period, with cost categories itemized.

Decision: Positive, stable contribution margins would support further diligence into overall profitability; unclear or negative margins would require cost-structure discussion with founders before valuation work.

Required input: Request cost-of-processing records (bank scheme fees, Wise currency-conversion costs, fraud/failure-handling costs) and the revenue ledger for GoCardless Ltd, same fiscal year and currency as above, split by plan tier.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S5](#source-s5)

> Is there a discount for charities?
> 
> Yes, we offer a 25% discount on some transaction fees for eligible charities and nonprofits. See terms
> 
> FAQs
> 
> How does GoCardless collect fees from me?
> 
> If you choose a Standard, Advanced or Pro plan, fees are deducted from your submitted payments. If you arrange a Custom plan with us, you’ll be invoiced monthly instead.
> 
> Are there any setup, monthly, or hidden fees?
> 
> No, for most GoCardless users there are no setup or monthly costs, and there are no hidden fees. You can sign up for free and pay as you go, with fees charged per transaction. Some add-on functionality is billed differently, but this is noted above.
> 
> Custom plans, however, come with a tailored pricing agreement. This could include relevant setup or monthly costs, but typically comes with discounted rates due to high transaction volumes or amounts. Chat with our friendly Sales team to see what we can offer you. Contact Sales
> 
> Can I change my plan or cancel any time?
> 
> Yes, you can change your plan or close your GoCardless account whenever you like. Our Standard, Advanced and Pro plans are all monthly rolling contracts designed for flexibility.
> 
> You can upgrade or downgrade your plan any time directly from your GoCardless dashboard. This must be done at least two working days before the end of the month to come into effect from the 1st of the following month. You'll be charged any applicable fees of your current pricing plan until the change is actioned.
> 
> Custom plans are subject to different terms and conditions that you'll be able to discuss with our Sales team. Contact Sales
> 
> Why does GoCardless charge VAT on its fees?
> 
> It's the VAT status of a supplier that determines whether VAT is charged, and not the VAT status of the customer. In April 2020, HMRC confirmed GoCardless was providing a VATable service, so we need to charge VAT on all transaction fees invoiced to UK merchants. This applies regardless of whether the business collecting the payment is VAT exempt or not. This is a HMRC decision based on UK VAT law and not something that we have any control over.
> 
> You may be eligible to claim back from HMRC the VAT charged on your fees, however we are unable to provide any tax advice and — as tax law can be complex — we recommend consulting with a qualified tax professional if you are unsure how the laws apply to your business.
> 
> Do I need separate bank accounts to collect international payments?
> 
> No, you don't need separate bank accounts to collect international payments with GoCardless. You can collect payments from 30+ countries around the world right into your UK bank account, with currency conversion powered by Wise.
