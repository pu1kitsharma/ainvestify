# Bounded research reliability check

Use the original retained Resend evidence, never an edited reference answer. One installed local model, at most 120 seconds, six logical calls, one correction per task; no live source fetch or model sweep. This is a development retest, not an unseen or practitioner result.

Accept only nine complete linked sections, a concise business/founder opening identifying the email offering and developer customer rather than a pricing footer/testimonial, complete published pricing context and qualifications, relevant customer event, and a concrete future offer requesting income/cost/customer records. Reject unsupported business performance, claimed independent verification, invalid comparisons or assumed execution. Inspect output separately from automatic model review. Preserve every result regardless of outcome.

Offline financial regressions must block double deduction of service costs, overhead mixed with variable costs and restricted/customer cash coverage, retain source/input IDs, and keep unambiguous partner netting and explicit exclusions usable. These rules do not verify company accounts.
