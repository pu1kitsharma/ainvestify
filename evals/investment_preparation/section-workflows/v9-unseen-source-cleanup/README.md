## Implemented fixes

- Preserve whole HTML blocks and adjacent context instead of splitting source sentences at a character count. Oversized blocks are explicitly omitted; footer entity/fee qualifications remain.
- Exclude structural menus and exact navigation controls while retaining observed destinations and annual/monthly pricing controls.
- Prefer complete current-format captures over older flattened claims from the same URL; keep originals in company evidence/history.
- Share the context budget across captured pages, prioritizing opening pricing and closing terms. Direct observed pricing links survive the link cap and take precedence over incidental fee calculators.
- Render quotations as separate attributed blocks and keep one source appendix in Markdown exports instead of duplicating sources under every action.
- Reject an order event without necessary ordering/purchase/trading evidence and an offer that omits cost/expense inputs while promising contribution analysis. These necessary checks do not establish every event's meaning or guarantee a sufficient record request.
- Remove the literal invitation instruction label mechanically; preserve the original response in attempt logs. Revalidate saved content against its shared analysis before publication.
- Scope offer-only repairs to the intended work contract and citation IDs, preventing an unrelated opening page from becoming the repair text.
- Include collection in the evaluator's deadline, retain acquired input records and prevent report overwrites.
