### Opportunity and unresolved risks

Status: complete

Reason to engage: If retained income from Pay-As-You-Drive Lease covers attributable variable delivery costs and eligible Pay-As-You-Drive Lease customers record completed delivery, the evidence could support further diligence on a repeatable business. Both conditions require reconciled company records.

Unresolved risk: The commercial case weakens if eligible Pay-As-You-Drive Lease customers do not record completed delivery. Equally observed cohorts are needed to identify participation gaps; event logs alone will not establish why customers behave that way.

Next decision: Resolve the linked contribution and customer-use questions before recommending an engagement or expansion; investigate adverse results and leave missing inputs unresolved.
