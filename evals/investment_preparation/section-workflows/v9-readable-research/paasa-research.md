### Revenue mechanism

Status: complete

Revenue mechanism: Published source summary: Customers pay for self-directed trading via the Access plan with no recurring fee, or for managed advisory services via the Apex plan which charges an annual percentage fee based on assets under management. Payment for the Apex plan occurs through automatic monthly deductions from the customer's wallet, while brokerage fees for trades are deducted at execution.

Unknown economics: What income does the investment entity retain from Apex Plan, and does it cover attributable variable service costs? Published statements do not establish realized income; reconcile the contracting entity, recognition and company-held cost records.

Sources: [S1](#source-s1), [S4](#source-s4)

### Opportunity and unresolved risks

Status: complete

Reason to engage: If retained income from Apex Plan covers attributable variable delivery costs and eligible Apex Plan customers record enrollment, the evidence could support further diligence on a repeatable business. Both conditions require reconciled company records.

Unresolved risk: The commercial case weakens if the variable cost of delivering Apex Plan consumes its retained income. The proposed reconciliation will distinguish partner charges and delivery costs before identifying pricing or cost work.

Next decision: Resolve the linked contribution and customer-use questions before recommending an engagement or expansion; investigate adverse results and leave missing inputs unresolved.
