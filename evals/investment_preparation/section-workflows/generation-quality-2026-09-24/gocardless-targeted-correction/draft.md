# GoCardless — company preparation

Internal AI drafts. Sources are reported claims; proposals have not been executed.

## Research memo

### Business and customer

Status: complete

GoCardless operates a payments platform letting businesses collect one-off, recurring and instalment payments via bank debit ('Pay by Bank'/Direct Debit) and send outbound payments, aiming to reduce card fees, payment failures and late-payment chasing for merchants of all sizes.

Sources: [S1](#source-s1), [S3](#source-s3)

### Revenue mechanism

Status: complete

Revenue mechanism: Standard, Advanced and Pro plans charge a percentage plus a fixed amount per transaction, deducted directly from submitted payments; UK fees also carry VAT. Custom plans (for higher-revenue or bespoke merchants) are invoiced monthly under negotiated, volume-discounted terms, and eligible charities receive a stated discount on some transaction fees.

Unknown economics: No earned revenue, cost, or margin figures were supplied; the disclosed processed payment volume is customer payment throughput, not GoCardless's recognized revenue, so a revenue ledger and cost records are needed.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4), [S5](#source-s5)

### Opportunity and unresolved risks

Status: complete

Reason to engage: If GoCardless's actual fee income scales consistently with its stated per-transaction schedule across the reported growth in merchant count, that could be a conditional signal worth reviewing further, though customer counts alone do not establish revenue or financial health.

Unresolved risk: The business depends on merchants switching from cards to bank-debit payments and on partner integrations to deliver its service to the reported customer base; growth in the number of businesses served does not by itself establish that per-merchant fee revenue or retention has held up over time.

Next decision: Whether to request financial statements and underlying billing records for deeper review; evidence of declining per-merchant revenue or unreconciled fee gaps would pause further diligence.

Sources: [S1](#source-s1), [S6](#source-s6)

## Founder proposal

### Why we are approaching you

Status: complete

Reported observation: Source materials report GoCardless passing 50,000 businesses served around 2019 and later describing over 100,000 businesses trusting the platform globally.

Question to explore: Has fee revenue per merchant grown alongside this customer count, or has growth diluted per-merchant economics?

Sources: [S1](#source-s1), [S6](#source-s6)

### The assistance we propose

Status: complete

Proposed work: An outside advisory team could reconcile GoCardless's published per-transaction pricing (percentage plus fixed fee, VAT, charity discounts, custom-plan terms) against actual invoices, credits and the revenue ledger for an agreed period, then build a cost-to-revenue margin bridge by plan tier using processing and partner cost records. Together these would clarify revenue predictability and contribution margin ahead of any further investment conversation, without assuming customer growth alone signals financial health.

Invitation: We'd welcome a conversation with the founders before requesting any sensitive billing, cost or agreement records.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4)

## Diligence request

### First diligence request

Status: complete

Question: Does actual transaction fee revenue reconcile with the published pricing schedule?

Records to request: GoCardless Ltd invoices, credits, signed custom-plan agreements, and transaction-level billing data for an agreed period (e.g. FY2025, GBP), plus the revenue recognition policy and revenue ledger.

Decision: Informs whether the standard/custom plan mix and discounting are understood well enough to trust revenue predictability before further diligence.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4)

### Second diligence request

Status: complete

Question: What do processing and partner costs imply about contribution margin by plan?

Records to request: GoCardless Ltd cost ledger for processing/partner fees (e.g. bank, scheme, FX), partner revenue-share agreements, the revenue recognition policy, and the earned-revenue ledger for the same agreed period, entity and currency as fee revenue.

Decision: Informs whether unit economics support further investment discussion, separate from transaction volume growth alone.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S5](#source-s5)

## Investment-readiness plan

### First readiness action

Status: complete

Action: Apply the full schedule—percentage, fixed fee, VAT treatment, and charity discount—to the same underlying transactions used in the ledger, then compare to recognized revenue for that period. Timing differences between billing and recognition are not automatically errors and should be separately explained.

Output: A fee reconciliation memo comparing schedule-implied fees to recognized revenue for the agreed period.

Decision: Informs whether the standard/custom plan mix and discounting are understood well enough to trust revenue predictability before further diligence.

Required input: GoCardless Ltd invoices, credits, signed custom-plan agreements, and transaction-level billing data for an agreed period (e.g. FY2025, GBP), plus the revenue recognition policy and revenue ledger.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4)

### Second readiness action

Status: complete

Action: Match attributable processing and partner costs to earned fee revenue for the same transactions and period to estimate contribution margin per plan tier. This is not 'net revenue' unless an established accounting policy defines it that way.

Output: A margin bridge showing earned revenue less attributable costs by plan tier for the agreed period.

Decision: Informs whether unit economics support further investment discussion, separate from transaction volume growth alone.

Required input: GoCardless Ltd cost ledger for processing/partner fees (e.g. bank, scheme, FX), partner revenue-share agreements, the revenue recognition policy, and the earned-revenue ledger for the same agreed period, entity and currency as fee revenue.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S5](#source-s5)

### S4 — commercial terms

Source: https://gocardless.com/pricing · retrieved 2026-09-15T04:44:02.316165+00:00 · observed not provided

> Is there a discount for charities?
> 
> Yes, we offer a 25% discount on some transaction fees for eligible charities and nonprofits. See terms
> 
> FAQs
> 
> How does GoCardless collect fees from me?
> 
> If you choose a Standard, Advanced or Pro plan, fees are deducted from your submitted payments. If you arrange a Custom plan with us, you’ll be invoiced monthly instead.
> 
> Are there any setup, monthly, or hidden fees?
> 
> No, for most GoCardless users there are no setup or monthly costs, and there are no hidden fees. You can sign up for free and pay as you go, with fees charged per transaction. Some add-on functionality is billed differently, but this is noted above.
> 
> Custom plans, however, come with a tailored pricing agreement. This could include relevant setup or monthly costs, but typically comes with discounted rates due to high transaction volumes or amounts. Chat with our friendly Sales team to see what we can offer you. Contact Sales
> 
> Can I change my plan or cancel any time?
> 
> Yes, you can change your plan or close your GoCardless account whenever you like. Our Standard, Advanced and Pro plans are all monthly rolling contracts designed for flexibility.
> 
> You can upgrade or downgrade your plan any time directly from your GoCardless dashboard. This must be done at least two working days before the end of the month to come into effect from the 1st of the following month. You'll be charged any applicable fees of your current pricing plan until the change is actioned.
> 
> Custom plans are subject to different terms and conditions that you'll be able to discuss with our Sales team. Contact Sales
> 
> Why does GoCardless charge VAT on its fees?
> 
> It's the VAT status of a supplier that determines whether VAT is charged, and not the VAT status of the customer. In April 2020, HMRC confirmed GoCardless was providing a VATable service, so we need to charge VAT on all transaction fees invoiced to UK merchants. This applies regardless of whether the business collecting the payment is VAT exempt or not. This is a HMRC decision based on UK VAT law and not something that we have any control over.
> 
> You may be eligible to claim back from HMRC the VAT charged on your fees, however we are unable to provide any tax advice and — as tax law can be complex — we recommend consulting with a qualified tax professional if you are unsure how the laws apply to your business.
> 
> Do I need separate bank accounts to collect international payments?
> 
> No, you don't need separate bank accounts to collect international payments with GoCardless. You can collect payments from 30+ countries around the world right into your UK bank account, with currency conversion powered by Wise.
