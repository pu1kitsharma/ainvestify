# GoCardless — company preparation

Internal AI drafts. Sources are reported claims; proposals have not been executed.

## Research memo

### Business and customer

Status: complete

GoCardless operates a bank-payment platform letting businesses collect one-off and recurring payments (e.g., via Direct Debit) directly from customer bank accounts, and send outbound payments, aiming to reduce card fees and payment failures for merchants of varying sizes.

Sources: [S1](#source-s1), [S3](#source-s3), [S5](#source-s5)

### Revenue mechanism

Status: complete

Revenue mechanism: Merchants choose Standard, Advanced or Pro plans, each charging a percentage plus fixed amount per transaction, deducted from submitted payments; larger merchants can negotiate Custom plans with volume discounts, invoiced monthly instead. Charities receive a stated discount on some fees. UK merchants are charged VAT on fees per HMRC ruling, separate from the underlying transaction fee.

Unknown economics: No recognized revenue, cost, or margin figures are supplied; request the revenue ledger, recognition policy, cost records, and Custom-plan agreements to determine actual earned revenue and profitability.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4)

### Opportunity and unresolved risks

Status: complete

Reason to engage: If GoCardless can show that its published or negotiated per-transaction fees translate into growing, reconciled recognized revenue with positive contribution after processing costs, this scale (100,000+ businesses, $130bn processed annually) could support further diligence.

Unresolved risk: Processed payment volume is not company revenue; the take rate merchants actually pay after discounts, caps, and Custom-plan terms is unknown, so scale claims alone cannot indicate financial health.

Next decision: Whether to proceed to a fuller financial review depends on founders providing revenue, cost and billing records; absent these, no conclusion on margin or sustainability can be drawn.

Sources: [S5](#source-s5), [S6](#source-s6)

## Founder proposal

### Why we are approaching you

Status: complete

Reported observation: GoCardless reports processing over $130bn in payments annually for 100,000+ businesses across 30+ countries, figures describing payment volume and customer count rather than company revenue.

Question to explore: What portion of this processed volume translates into GoCardless's own recognized fee revenue and profit?

Sources: [S5](#source-s5)

### The assistance we propose

Status: complete

Proposed work: We'd like to help reconcile GoCardless's published and Custom-plan fee structures against actual billed and recognized revenue, and separately assess margin after processing and partner costs, using agreed periods and the UK entity's records. This would produce a fee reconciliation schedule and a margin bridge to inform whether the business's scale is matched by measurable, sustainable earned revenue and contribution.

Invitation: Before requesting any sensitive financial records, we'd welcome a conversation with the founders to agree on scope, periods, and entities.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S5](#source-s5)

## Diligence request

### First diligence request

Status: complete

Question: Does actual billed revenue match the published fee schedule across plans?

Records to request: Revenue ledger, transaction-level invoices and credits, signed Custom-plan agreements, and the revenue recognition policy (including treatment of costs already deducted before invoicing) for GoCardless Ltd, one agreed period in GBP, covering Standard, Advanced, Pro and Custom merchants over the same transaction set.

Decision: Confirms whether pricing is applied consistently; material unexplained gaps would raise billing or disclosure concerns before further engagement.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4)

### Second diligence request

Status: complete

Question: What margin does GoCardless earn after processing and partner costs?

Records to request: Income statement or management accounts, revenue ledger, revenue recognition policy, partner/processing cost breakdown (bank scheme, FX/Wise costs), and operating expense detail for GoCardless Ltd for two consecutive agreed fiscal periods in GBP, to be confirmed with founders.

Decision: Determines whether unit economics support the observed scale; a negative contribution margin repeated across both agreed periods would be a material concern requiring further explanation before deeper engagement.

Sources: [S5](#source-s5), [S6](#source-s6)

## Investment-readiness plan

### First readiness action

Status: complete

Action: Apply each plan's full fee structure (fixed fee, percentage, discounts, VAT treatment) to the same underlying transactions to compute expected fees, then reconcile against actual invoiced and deducted amounts. Timing differences between billing and recognition are not automatically errors and should be separately explained using the revenue recognition policy.

Output: A reconciliation schedule comparing expected versus billed and recognized fee revenue by plan type for the agreed period.

Decision: Confirms whether pricing is applied consistently; material unexplained gaps would raise billing or disclosure concerns before further engagement.

Required input: Revenue ledger, transaction-level invoices and credits, signed Custom-plan agreements, and the revenue recognition policy (including treatment of costs already deducted before invoicing) for GoCardless Ltd, one agreed period in GBP, covering Standard, Advanced, Pro and Custom merchants over the same transaction set.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4)

### Second readiness action

Status: complete

Action: Subtract attributable processing and partner costs from reconciled fee revenue for two consecutive agreed periods to estimate contribution margin each period, then compare the two results side by side. Keep this separate from any reported figure not defined by an accounting policy, since cash collected is not the same as recognized revenue.

Output: A margin bridge from recognized fee revenue to contribution after direct costs for the agreed period.

Decision: Determines whether unit economics support the observed scale; a negative contribution margin repeated across both agreed periods would be a material concern requiring further explanation before deeper engagement.

Required input: Income statement or management accounts, revenue ledger, revenue recognition policy, partner/processing cost breakdown (bank scheme, FX/Wise costs), and operating expense detail for GoCardless Ltd for two consecutive agreed fiscal periods in GBP, to be confirmed with founders.

Sources: [S5](#source-s5), [S6](#source-s6)

> FAQs
> 
> How does GoCardless collect fees from me?
> 
> If you choose a Standard, Advanced or Pro plan, fees are deducted from your submitted payments. If you arrange a Custom plan with us, you’ll be invoiced monthly instead.
> 
> Are there any setup, monthly, or hidden fees?
> 
> No, for most GoCardless users there are no setup or monthly costs, and there are no hidden fees. You can sign up for free and pay as you go, with fees charged per transaction. Some add-on functionality is billed differently, but this is noted above.
> 
> Custom plans, however, come with a tailored pricing agreement. This could include relevant setup or monthly costs, but typically comes with discounted rates due to high transaction volumes or amounts. Chat with our friendly Sales team to see what we can offer you. Contact Sales
> 
> Can I change my plan or cancel any time?
> 
> Yes, you can change your plan or close your GoCardless account whenever you like. Our Standard, Advanced and Pro plans are all monthly rolling contracts designed for flexibility.
> 
> You can upgrade or downgrade your plan any time directly from your GoCardless dashboard. This must be done at least two working days before the end of the month to come into effect from the 1st of the following month. You'll be charged any applicable fees of your current pricing plan until the change is actioned.
> 
> Custom plans are subject to different terms and conditions that you'll be able to discuss with our Sales team. Contact Sales
> 
> Why does GoCardless charge VAT on its fees?
> 
> It's the VAT status of a supplier that determines whether VAT is charged, and not the VAT status of the customer. In April 2020, HMRC confirmed GoCardless was providing a VATable service, so we need to charge VAT on all transaction fees invoiced to UK merchants. This applies regardless of whether the business collecting the payment is VAT exempt or not. This is a HMRC decision based on UK VAT law and not something that we have any control over.
> 
> You may be eligible to claim back from HMRC the VAT charged on your fees, however we are unable to provide any tax advice and — as tax law can be complex — we recommend consulting with a qualified tax professional if you are unsure how the laws apply to your business.
> 
> Do I need separate bank accounts to collect international payments?
> 
> No, you don't need separate bank accounts to collect international payments with GoCardless. You can collect payments from 30+ countries around the world right into your UK bank account, with currency conversion powered by Wise.
