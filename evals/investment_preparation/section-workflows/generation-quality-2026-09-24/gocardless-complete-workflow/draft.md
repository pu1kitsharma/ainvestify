# GoCardless — company preparation

Internal AI drafts. Sources are reported claims; proposals have not been executed.

## Research memo

### Business and customer

Status: complete

GoCardless operates a bank-payment platform used by 100,000+ businesses to collect one-off, recurring and outbound payments via Direct Debit and Pay by Bank, replacing higher-cost card payments and reducing failed collections for its merchant customers.

Sources: [S1](#source-s1), [S3](#source-s3), [S5](#source-s5)

### Revenue mechanism

Status: complete

Revenue mechanism: Standard, Advanced and Pro plan merchants are charged a per-transaction fee combining a percentage and a fixed amount, deducted directly from submitted payments; UK fees carry VAT. Custom plans (for larger or bespoke merchants) are invoiced monthly under negotiated, volume-discounted terms, and eligible charities receive a discount on some transaction fees.

Unknown economics: The $130bn figure is payment volume processed for customers, not company revenue; actual earned fee revenue and attributable processing/partner costs by period are not supplied.

Sources: [S5](#source-s5), [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4)

### Opportunity and unresolved risks

Status: complete

Reason to engage: GoCardless runs a bank-payment platform used by a large merchant base, with a published per-transaction tariff and custom pricing for larger accounts; worth investigating whether earned fee revenue and margins after processing costs actually scale with its customer base, though this is not established from public claims alone.

Unresolved risk: Reliance on volume-based custom pricing discounts for larger merchants could compress the effective take rate as customers move off standard tiers, and public tariffs alone cannot show whether this erodes contribution margin.

Next decision: Whether to proceed to detailed financial diligence; request actual fee revenue, invoices, credits and attributable processing/partner costs by an agreed period. Evidence of thin or declining contribution margins would pause further engagement pending clarification.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4)

## Founder proposal

### Why we are approaching you

Status: complete

Reported observation: GoCardless publicly reports processing over $130bn in payments annually for 100,000+ businesses, alongside a published per-transaction pricing schedule with tiered plans and a charity discount.

Question to explore: How does this published volume and tariff translate into GoCardless's actual earned revenue and margin?

Sources: [S5](#source-s5), [S1](#source-s1), [S2](#source-s2)

### The assistance we propose

Status: complete

Proposed work: We would like to reconcile GoCardless's published fee schedule against actual billed and recognized revenue for an agreed period, and separately assess contribution margin after processing and partner costs. This would produce a fee reconciliation table and a margin bridge to help clarify revenue quality and unit economics ahead of any investment discussion.

Invitation: Before requesting any financial records, we would welcome a conversation with the founders to agree the right entity, period and data scope.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S5](#source-s5)

## Diligence request

### First diligence request

Status: complete

Question: Does actual fee revenue match the published tariff once discounts and VAT are applied?

Records to request: Transaction-level billing data (plan type, transaction count, average value, VAT treatment, charity discount flags), invoices and credit notes for GoCardless Ltd, agreed period (e.g., one fiscal year), in GBP.

Decision: Informs whether GoCardless's realized take rate matches its public pricing or is materially eroded by custom-plan discounts, informing revenue quality assessment.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4)

### Second diligence request

Status: complete

Question: What is contribution margin after processing and partner costs, by period?

Records to request: Entity-level revenue ledger and cost breakdown (partner/scheme processing costs, operating expenses) for GoCardless Ltd, for an agreed multi-period window, in GBP.

Decision: Informs whether unit economics are improving, stable or deteriorating; a single loss-making period alone cannot establish sustainability either way.

Sources: [S5](#source-s5), [S2](#source-s2)

## Investment-readiness plan

### First readiness action

Status: complete

Action: Apply the full published schedule (percentage plus fixed fee, VAT exclusion, charity discount) to the same set of transactions used for actual billing, then reconcile expected fees against invoiced amounts and the revenue ledger. Timing differences between billing and recognition should be distinguished from actual pricing discrepancies.

Output: A reconciliation table comparing schedule-implied fees, invoiced fees and recognized revenue by plan tier for the agreed period.

Decision: Informs whether GoCardless's realized take rate matches its public pricing or is materially eroded by custom-plan discounts, informing revenue quality assessment.

Required input: Transaction-level billing data (plan type, transaction count, average value, VAT treatment, charity discount flags), invoices and credit notes for GoCardless Ltd, agreed period (e.g., one fiscal year), in GBP.

Sources: [S2](#source-s2), [S4](#source-s4)

### Second readiness action

Status: complete

Action: Subtract attributable processing and partner costs from recognized fee revenue for each period under the company's stated accounting policy to derive contribution margin. Compare the trend across periods rather than drawing conclusions from a single period's result.

Output: A margin bridge showing recognized revenue, attributable costs and contribution by period.

Decision: Informs whether unit economics are improving, stable or deteriorating; a single loss-making period alone cannot establish sustainability either way.

Required input: Entity-level revenue ledger and cost breakdown (partner/scheme processing costs, operating expenses) for GoCardless Ltd, for an agreed multi-period window, in GBP.

Sources: [S5](#source-s5), [S2](#source-s2)

> Is there a discount for charities?

> FAQs
> 
> How does GoCardless collect fees from me?
> 
> If you choose a Standard, Advanced or Pro plan, fees are deducted from your submitted payments. If you arrange a Custom plan with us, you’ll be invoiced monthly instead.
> 
> Are there any setup, monthly, or hidden fees?
> 
> No, for most GoCardless users there are no setup or monthly costs, and there are no hidden fees. You can sign up for free and pay as you go, with fees charged per transaction. Some add-on functionality is billed differently, but this is noted above.
> 
> Custom plans, however, come with a tailored pricing agreement. This could include relevant setup or monthly costs, but typically comes with discounted rates due to high transaction volumes or amounts. Chat with our friendly Sales team to see what we can offer you. Contact Sales
> 
> Can I change my plan or cancel any time?
> 
> Yes, you can change your plan or close your GoCardless account whenever you like. Our Standard, Advanced and Pro plans are all monthly rolling contracts designed for flexibility.
> 
> You can upgrade or downgrade your plan any time directly from your GoCardless dashboard. This must be done at least two working days before the end of the month to come into effect from the 1st of the following month. You'll be charged any applicable fees of your current pricing plan until the change is actioned.
> 
> Custom plans are subject to different terms and conditions that you'll be able to discuss with our Sales team. Contact Sales
> 
> Why does GoCardless charge VAT on its fees?
> 
> It's the VAT status of a supplier that determines whether VAT is charged, and not the VAT status of the customer. In April 2020, HMRC confirmed GoCardless was providing a VATable service, so we need to charge VAT on all transaction fees invoiced to UK merchants. This applies regardless of whether the business collecting the payment is VAT exempt or not. This is a HMRC decision based on UK VAT law and not something that we have any control over.
> 
> You may be eligible to claim back from HMRC the VAT charged on your fees, however we are unable to provide any tax advice and — as tax law can be complex — we recommend consulting with a qualified tax professional if you are unsure how the laws apply to your business.
> 
> Do I need separate bank accounts to collect international payments?
> 
> No, you don't need separate bank accounts to collect international payments with GoCardless. You can collect payments from 30+ countries around the world right into your UK bank account, with currency conversion powered by Wise.
